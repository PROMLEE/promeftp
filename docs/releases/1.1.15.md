# PromeFTP 1.1.15

## What's new

- Windows: **File → New Window** (⌘⇧N) opens another main window. Opening PromeFTP again while it runs, or clicking its Dock icon when no window is open, also opens one. Each SSH, SFTP, and agent tab belongs to the window that opened it, and closing a window ends its sessions. Bookmarks, workspaces, and connection history changed in one window appear in the others.
- SFTP: press **⌘F** or the magnifier in a panel to filter the folder on screen by name. Every word must appear in the name, case does not matter, and `*` or `?` match a whole name, as in `*.log`. Esc clears the filter, and Esc again closes it. The filter only hides rows; it does not search subfolders.
- SFTP: each panel shows its most used buttons and keeps the rest in the **More** menu (…). Right-click a panel's title bar to show or hide buttons, or choose **Customize Toolbar…** to also change their order. Local and remote panels each have their own setting. New folders, splits, hidden files, and folder comparison now start in the More menu; **Restore Defaults** brings back the original set.

## Known issues

- Korean, Chinese, and Japanese text in the terminal is wide with the default Menlo font. Choose a monospaced font that includes these characters in Settings → Terminal.
- Some labels in Korean, Japanese, and Chinese are machine translated and may read awkwardly.

## Update notes

- Close active sessions before updating.
- Requires macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon.
